# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vue 3 + TypeScript, confirmed by the user. Vite is used for the build, Vue Router for navigation, Pinia for shared UI state, and Phosphor Icons for a consistent icon system.

## Users

Primary users are Zhejiang cultural-tourism content operators, project creators, and reviewers. They create short city-promotion videos, inspect generated content, manage revisions, complete compliance review, and export platform-specific deliverables.

## Product Purpose

“浙旅智创” turns a structured tourism communication brief into a traceable 15-60 second short-video production workflow. It supports fast creation, professional node editing, human review, and multi-platform export.

## Positioning

The product combines Zhejiang-specific cultural knowledge, multi-agent generation, source traceability, copyright records, node-level regeneration, and platform adaptation in one production workspace.

## Operating Context

The primary workflow is: choose a scene, configure audience and style, attach materials, generate copy and storyboards, inspect media nodes, review factual and copyright risks, approve a version, and export channel variants.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Project states include draft, generating, pending review, approved, rejected, and archived.
- Creation supports scenic recommendation, city branding, festival campaigns, and other templates.
- Parameters include city, theme, audience, regional style, duration, target platform, and aspect ratio.
- Professional mode exposes copy, storyboard, media, style, and final-version nodes with partial regeneration.
- Review includes factual, cultural-expression, content-safety, and copyright checks.
- Exports include video, cover, subtitles, and copyright materials for multiple platforms.
- This frontend uses typed mock services and illustrative data until backend APIs are supplied.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: 浙旅智创.
- Preserve the provided design's Jiangnan landscape imagery, cool teal accent, white workspace surfaces, and clear left-navigation model.
- The experience must feel calm, credible, locally rooted, and suitable for professional public-cultural communication.

## Product Principles

- Keep every generated result traceable to its facts, source materials, model, and version.
- Make complex generation progress understandable at a glance.
- Preserve user work through drafts, retries, and partial regeneration.
- Put review and export readiness ahead of decorative presentation.
- Let Zhejiang's place identity appear through imagery and language without weakening operational clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, semantic form labels, responsive layouts, and readable status language are required across the product.
